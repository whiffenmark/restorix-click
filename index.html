The Challenge

Property Damage Gets Stressful Fast

A leak, flood, fire, storm, or mold issue can turn into a bigger problem if it is ignored. The goal of Restorix is to help people understand what may need to happen next and what kind of restoration help to look for.

Stop the Damage

The first priority in any restoration situation is limiting further damage before more serious problems develop.

Understand the Risk

Water, fire, mold, and sewage situations each carry different risks. Knowing what you are dealing with helps you make better decisions.

Find the Right Help

Not every restoration problem is the same. Restorix is designed to help connect people with the type of help that fits their specific situation.
